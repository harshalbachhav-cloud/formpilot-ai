FormPilot AI

Your review-first Google Forms companion

Free starter · Privacy-first

1 · Open your Google Form

Paste a form link shared in your class group. This app opens the form for you. For privacy and browser security, it does not secretly read form contents.

2 · Add optional context

Use only details that are relevant. Don't add passwords, private IDs, or facts you don't want stored on this device.

3 · Add questions and prepare draft answers

This starter does not connect to a paid AI service. It creates cautious editable templates. Ratings and personal opinions must come from you. For automatic question reading and stronger AI drafts, a backend/model integration is needed.

4 · Copy answers and review

Approve only answers that reflect your real experience. Copy each answer into the corresponding form field. Final submission stays in your hands.

Install on your phone

After this app is hosted on a secure HTTPS website, open that address in Android Chrome and choose ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app. This ZIP alone is not yet hosted, so installation will be available after deployment.

Android: publish the app to an HTTPS host, open the published URL in Chrome, tap ⋮, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On Windows, open the same URL in Chrome or Edge and use the install icon or browser menu.
